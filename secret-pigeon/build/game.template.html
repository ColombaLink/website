You are Agent Coo, a carrier pigeon with a radio. Fly over Basel, collect parts and put together a working LoRaWAN network, one level at a time.

The city is real: swisstopo’s 3D model of every building, the aerial photo underneath and Basel’s register of public trees. The radio network is made up for the game.
