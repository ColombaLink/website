Manufacturing & industry · Hardware + SaaS

Industrial IoT monitoring platform

An end-to-end monitoring service for manufacturing and industrial sites. We deliver the devices, the network and the platform, and the customer gets the data.

Outcome Issues are detected far sooner: response time dropped from hours to minutes. A complete alarm history makes hard-to-diagnose faults traceable.

Challenge

Production sites needed continuous temperature and humidity monitoring with reliable alarms. They had to get it without running cables, managing devices themselves or keeping a technician on hand for upkeep.

What we built

  • Custom battery-powered wireless sensors, designed from component selection to firmware, that run five years or more without recharging
  • A private, isolated LoRaWAN network, set up and managed for each site
  • Off-the-shelf LoRaWAN sensors integrated alongside our own: CO₂ and air quality, water leaks, desk occupancy, sound level, people counting and current clamps for energy monitoring
  • A platform running on infrastructure we operate ourselves, with live dashboards, multi-state alarms, per-project user roles and long-term data archiving
  • Open interfaces to third-party devices, applications and visualisation tools
  • Delivered as a subscription, including on-site evaluation, installation and maintenance
  • Embedded firmware
  • Private LoRaWAN
  • Self-hosted
  • Time-series data
  • React & TypeScript
  • Kubernetes
  • Terraform
  • Anomaly detection
Environment · Emergency coordination · Urban planning

Collaborative geospatial workspace

A shared workspace where teams bring maps, layers, field updates, comments and timelines together to make decisions about places.

Outcome One shared source of truth from field observation to decision. Teams compare change over time, coordinate live and present findings, all from the same workspace.

Challenge

Teams making spatial decisions worked across scattered files, map tools and message threads. Nobody had a single view of the evidence, the discussion or how either had changed over time.

What we built

  • Real-time, multi-user map rooms with presence, invitations and view/edit permissions
  • Import of GeoJSON, GeoPackage, Shapefile and GeoTIFF, with server-side tile pyramids and band, stretch and colour-map controls for rasters
  • Markers, drawings and threaded comments with @mentions that can be filtered and resolved
  • Full change history and offline editing through conflict-free (CRDT) synchronisation
  • Packaged as containers (Docker), so it can be deployed on your own servers
  • A presentation mode that turns curated map views into a guided story, plus public sharing, in four languages
  • Real-time sync (CRDT, WebSockets)
  • GIS: raster & vector
  • Rust & Python geoprocessing
  • Authentication & RBAC
  • PostgreSQL & Redis
  • Docker
  • Internationalisation
